Engineered a multi-modal agentic framework orchestrating custom tools for automated whole-slide image (WSI) analysis, including Visual Question Answering (VQA), report generation, and zero-shot classification and ROI segmentation.

**Key contributions:**
- Integrated state-of-the-art pathology foundation models (SlideChat, TITAN, CONCH, MUSK) to enable context-aware reasoning over gigapixel-scale images.
- Implemented a human-in-the-loop interface with a hierarchical k-means algorithm to iteratively refine model predictions for complex classification tasks.
- Ensured interpretability and clinical alignment throughout the pipeline.

**Technologies:** Python · PyTorch · LangChain · Vision-Language Models (VLM)

**Period:** Nov. 2025 – Present
