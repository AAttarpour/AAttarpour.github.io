MAPL3 (Mapping Axonal Projection in Light Sheet Microscopy in 3D) is a 3D computational pipeline for identifying individual axonal fibers and mapping their circuitry brain-wide at single-axon resolution.

**Key contributions:**
- Developed a novel DL architecture combining convolutional neural networks and vision transformers for axon segmentation.
- Deployed self-supervised approaches to improve model generalizability and reduce dependency on labeled data.
- Designed and implemented parallelized image processing pipelines for pre- and post-processing of large-scale light sheet microscopy data.
- Work submitted to *Nature Methods* (under review, Dec. 2025).

**Technologies:** Python · PyTorch · Bash · Self-supervised Learning · Vision Transformers

**Period:** Jan. 2023 – May 2025
